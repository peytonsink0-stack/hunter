About the Manga

Steel Ball Run is a serialized manga that follows an exciting horse race across the United States. It blends adventure, drama, and supernatural elements in a unique historical setting.

Setting & Tone

The story is set in the late 1800s and has a strong western atmosphere. The race itself crosses iconic landscapes, and the mood mixes action with mystery.
